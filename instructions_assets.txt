The site currently runs on an animated CSS/SVG radar hero (no photo/video
required to function) so it's usable today. Swap in real media as you shoot it,
using the specs below. Replace via the "assets" capability or by editing
the relevant file directly.

1) HERO VIDEO (highest priority)
   - Format: MP4 (H.264), 8–15 sec loop, muted/autoplay-safe, 1080x1920 (portrait
     crop will be applied on mobile) or 1920x1080 landscape master.
   - Content ideas, in order of impact:
       a) Armed response vehicle pulling up to a gate at night, blue light bar visible
       b) Officer doing an access-control check at an estate boom gate
       c) Control-room operator watching a bank of live monitors
   - Keep it under ~8MB compressed (site currently embeds media inline; anything
     larger should be hosted and referenced by URL instead of inlined).
   - Overlay-safe: avoid busy motion in the top-center 40% where the headline sits.

2) SERVICE PHOTOS (one hero image per service, 1600x1000 min, landscape)
   - Manned Guarding: officer at a gatehouse / reception desk, PSIRA badge visible
   - Armed Response: response vehicle in motion or parked with lights on
   - Electronic Security: technician installing a CCTV camera / control panel
   - Off-Site Monitoring: control room wall of screens, operator in frame
   - Specialized/VIP: close-protection officer beside a vehicle (no faces of
     actual clients — use a generic/model shot or silhouette for privacy)

3) ICONS
   - Current icons are hand-drawn inline SVG (lightweight, on-brand blue).
     No action needed unless you want a custom icon set to match your crest's
     eagle motif more literally (e.g. wing-shaped dividers).

4) LOGO
   - Already embedded as a base64 data URI in js/app.js (window.__MAREDI_LOGO__).
   - If you get a vector (SVG) version of the crest, swap it in for crisper
     scaling at large hero sizes.

5) TRUST/PROOF ASSETS (optional but high-converting)
   - PSIRA registration certificate (photographed or scanned) for a "verified"
     badge section
   - 3–5 short client testimonial clips or quotes (estate manager, business owner)
   - Photos of your actual fleet/vehicles for the "deployment strength" band

DELIVERY

     the published page directly — no rebuild needed.
